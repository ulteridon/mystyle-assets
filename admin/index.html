Images & Categories

Everything here controls what the app shows. Hide a category or a single image and it disappears from the phone after the next app launch (once published). Add images to a category — or create a new category — and they appear in the app.

Templates

Photo-poster templates (templates_db.json). Hide, edit layout, or create new ones. The layout editor sets where the user's photo and name go.

Captions

Caption variations overlaid on Images-tab cards (imageTexts.json). Each line becomes its own shareable card in the app.

Publish

Commits the changed files to . The app picks them up on next launch (raw.githubusercontent caches for ~5 minutes).
